How the maths works

Raw points available in each category don't match the category weight — Revenue has 35 raw against a 20-point weight, Founder Mindset 30 against 25. This tool scores raw within the category, then scales to the weight.

Formula

Weighted = (raw ÷ raw max) × weight. Negative raw totals clamp to zero. The six weighted scores sum to 100.

Tier thresholds

Decision logic — know this cold before every session

How the tool decides

1

Blockers run first

Operations at Critical stops everything — unresolved operations blocks every other growth lever. Profitability at Critical forces a margin review before any traffic scaling. Neither is a Forge Track™; both are pre-work.

2

Pressure × revenue impact

Each remaining area scores pressure level (Stable 0, Medium 1, High 2, Critical 3) multiplied by its revenue impact weight — Conversion 1.00, Traffic 0.85, Campaign Execution 0.80, Basket 0.70. Highest product wins.

3

One track activates

The winner becomes the active Forge Track™. The runner-up is logged, not ignored — that is what you name if the client pushes to fix multiple things at once.

4

You still decide

The tool produces the default reading of the data. If you have context the numbers don't hold, override the ratings. EcomForges decides the track — not the client, and not the calculator.

Pushback response — one version, no alternatives
Activating two tracks simultaneously dilutes both — you get movement on nothing. What we're doing is sequencing, not ignoring. The second issue is logged. It activates when this one is resolved.

How the health score is built

Weights

  • Execution rate — 35
  • KPI targets met — 20
  • Dependency load — 15
  • Decision-maker attendance — 15
  • Payment reliability — 15

Why execution is heaviest

The doctrine is that we provide direction and execution determines Forge Output™. If execution is the product, it has to be the heaviest measure — the same way Founder Mindset is the heaviest category at qualification.

Hard triggers override the score. A seller can hold a respectable health number and still exit on a trigger — three consecutive dead cycles is a pattern, not a bad month.

Floor rules — why the number alone is not enough

  • Attendance, payment, and low dependency are worth 45 points between them. A client can bank all of it without executing a single directive, which would read as healthy.
  • Execution under 70% caps the outcome at Coach — at least one directive per cycle is not landing, and that has to be named.
  • Execution under 50% caps it at Downgrade — more than half the product is going unused.
  • Any warning trigger caps it at Coach. A warning means there is something to say in session; Continue means saying nothing.

Delivering the outcome

Each outcome generates the script in EcomForges language — neutral, precise, no apology, no motivation. Say it once. Do not negotiate it in the session.
