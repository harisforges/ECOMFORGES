# Data request — EcomForges growth review

Send this list to the client before a cycle. It is written for them to read directly.

---

To prepare your growth review we need the numbers below. Most of them are on one or
two screens in your Seller Centre.

**Screenshots are fine.** So is a CSV export, or just typing the figures into a reply.
Do not spend time formatting anything.

### Period

- Which dates does this cover? (30 days is ideal)
- Did it include a major campaign day — 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12, Raya, payday week?

### Platform

- Which platform is this data from? (Shopee / Lazada / TikTok Shop / your own site)
- Which category are your main products listed under?

### Traffic and conversion

- Total sessions or visitors
- Total orders
- Conversion rate
- Share of traffic that is organic (not paid) — a rough % is fine

### Revenue

- Total GMV / revenue for the period
- Average order value (basket size)
- Roughly what % of the revenue came from campaign or promo days
- Your top 5 products, with units sold and revenue for each

### Money

- Total ad spend
- ROAS
- Gross margin % — after cost of goods, before ads

### Operations

Pick the one that describes the period best:

- Clean — no fulfilment problems
- Minor delays
- SLA breaches / late shipment penalties
- Out of stock on top-selling products

---

**If you do not have a figure, leave it out.** We would rather work with a gap we know
about than a number that was estimated to fill the form.

### Where to find them

- **Shopee** — Seller Centre → Business Insights → Overview (traffic, orders, CVR,
  sales); Marketing Centre → Ads for spend and ROAS
- **Lazada** — Seller Centre → Business Advisor → Dashboard and Product
- **TikTok Shop** — Seller Centre → Analytics → Overview and Product
- **Own website (Shopify / WooCommerce)** — Analytics → Overview; margin from your own
  cost records
