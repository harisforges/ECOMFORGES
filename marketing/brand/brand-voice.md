# Brand Voice

EcomForges sounds:
- Direct
- Intelligent
- Honest
- Practical
- Analytical
- Educational
- Slightly provocative
- Anti-BS

Avoid:
- Generic guru language
- Generic agency language
- Fake urgency/scarcity
- Overpromising
- “10X” style hype
- Excessive emojis
- Corporate jargon
- Fearmongering

BM should sound natural Malaysian conversational language.
English should be natural professional English, not literal translation.
