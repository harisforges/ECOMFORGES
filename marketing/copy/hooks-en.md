# Hooks — English

1. Your store has sales. But do you know why it’s still stuck?
2. Ads are running. Traffic is coming in. But do you know what’s actually broken?
3. If your answer to every sales drop is “increase the ad budget”, there may be a bigger problem.
4. RM499 may be cheaper than one wrong ecommerce decision.
5. If the answer to every ecommerce problem is “run more ads”, we don’t need consultants.
6. You don’t necessarily need more ecommerce tips. You may need a diagnosis.
7. High sales don’t automatically mean you have a healthy ecommerce business.
8. Traffic isn’t the problem if your real problem is conversion.
9. Ecommerce owners shouldn’t have to guess what’s happening inside their own store.
10. You know this month’s sales. But do you know WHY they look like that?
11. Don’t scale what you don’t understand.
12. Many sellers aren’t failing because they don’t work hard. They’re optimising the wrong thing.
13. If you’re already spending thousands on ads, the first question isn’t “how much more should we spend?”
14. The real question is: “What should we fix first?”
15. A store in Shah Alam doesn’t necessarily need the same strategy as one in Bangi.
16. Shopee, TikTok Shop and your own website create different problems and behaviours.
17. If you don’t understand your ecommerce numbers, it’s difficult to know whether your team or agency is doing the right thing.
18. Don’t confuse activity with progress.
19. There are hundreds of things you could do in ecommerce. What should you do first?
20. We don’t want to give you more work. We want to help you make better decisions.
