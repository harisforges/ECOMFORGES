# Facebook / Instagram — Primary Text, English

Built from `copy/hooks-en.md`, `copy/headlines-en.md` and `copy/cta-en.md`.
Every variant follows PAS and makes WIIFM explicit.

**Price wording.** These say *from RM499 a month*. RM499 is the entry point and
the full ladder is deliberately unpublished, so a bare flat price in a live ad
misrepresents the structure. Settled — see `marketing/RECONCILIATION.md`.

**Scope wording.** These say we read the data and your team executes. EcomForges
does not run ads, log into accounts or edit listings. Never write copy implying
otherwise.

**Compliance.** No guarantees, no percentages, no timeframes, no invented proof.
Meta rejects "guaranteed sales" claims, and under Malaysian consumer protection
law a misleading representation in trade is an offence.

## Variant 1 — Stuck store (cold, problem-aware)

**Hook:** Your store has sales. But do you know why it's still stuck?

Sales come in. Ads are running. And still the month ends without the business
feeling like it moved.

The usual next step is to raise the ad budget. But if the real constraint is
conversion, the offer or the product page, more traffic just brings more people
to the same problem — at a higher cost per order.

EcomForges reads your actual platform data and tells you which one it is, and
what to fix first. You keep control of your accounts; your team runs the moves.

Understand first. Fix second. Scale third.

From RM499 a month, month to month.

**CTA:** Want to know what you should fix first? WhatsApp us.

## Variant 2 — Cost of the wrong decision (money angle)

**Hook:** RM499 may be cheaper than one wrong ecommerce decision.

A month of budget aimed at the wrong problem costs more than a month of
knowing which problem it was.

Most stores are not short of things they could fix. They are short of an order
to fix them in — so ten things get half-done and nothing visibly moves.

We diagnose from your own numbers, name one constraint, and give you three
moves in order with a single metric attached. Next cycle we check whether that
number moved, and say so plainly if it didn't.

From RM499 a month. No lock-in, no notice period.

**CTA:** Don't increase the budget blindly. Diagnose first.

## Variant 3 — Anti-guru (challenging, for the tips-fatigued)

**Hook:** If the answer to every ecommerce problem is "run more ads", we don't
need consultants.

There is no one formula that fits every store. A seller doing RM30k a month
with a conversion problem and a seller doing RM30k with a margin problem need
opposite advice — and most content online gives them the same advice.

EcomForges starts from the condition of your store, not a list of 50 tips. We
explain why something is recommended, so your team can repeat it without us.

The goal isn't to make you dependent on a consultant. It's to make you a better
ecommerce decision-maker.

From RM499 a month.

**CTA:** Stop guessing. Start diagnosing.

## Variant 4 — Multi-channel benchmark (for sellers on 2+ platforms)

**Hook:** Shopee, TikTok Shop and your own website create different problems.

If you run the same catalogue on two platforms, you already own the most useful
comparison in your business and probably aren't using it.

Same products, same prices, same month. So when one platform converts worse
than the other, the gap isn't the market — it's the platform, the listing or
the campaign structure. That narrows the problem in a single afternoon.

We do that read with you, then hand your team the three moves that follow from
it.

From RM499 a month.

**CTA:** Let's look at what's actually happening in your ecommerce business.
