# Retargeting — English and BM

Retargeting talks to someone who has already seen the offer and not acted. The
temptation is to add urgency, and that is exactly what `brand/copywriting-rules.md`
forbids: no countdowns, no "last chance", no invented scarcity on a monthly
subscription that has neither a deadline nor a limited number of seats.

What replaces urgency is **specificity**. They didn't act because the value was
still abstract. So be concrete about what the first session produces.

## Audiences and what each one already knows

| Audience | Already saw | What the ad must add |
|---|---|---|
| Visited site, didn't enquire | The offer | What a session actually produces |
| Read the FAQ | The advisory scope | That the read comes before commitment |
| Started an enquiry, didn't send | Enough to hesitate | Removal of the effort objection |
| Past client, quiet 60+ days | Everything | A reason that isn't a discount |

Do not retarget people who explicitly declined. Under PDPA, and simply as a
matter of not being a nuisance, a declined lead is out of the pool.

---

## EN 1 — Visited, didn't enquire

> You looked at the RM499 page and didn't get in touch. Fair enough — here is
> what actually happens if you do.
>
> You send 30 days of platform data. We come back with which channel is
> underperforming and what that gap is worth. Then you decide whether the
> monthly work is worth it.
>
> The read comes first, and it costs you nothing to find out.
>
> **WhatsApp us today.**

## EN 2 — Read the FAQ, didn't act

> Still deciding? The honest version: we are advisers, not an agency. Nobody
> logs into your store, and your team runs every change.
>
> If what you need is someone to execute the work for you, an agency is the
> better fit and we will say so. If what you need is to know which work matters
> this month, that is the job.
>
> **Let's talk about your store.**

## EN 3 — Abandoned enquiry

> You started sending your numbers and stopped. If it was the effort — it is
> screenshots of the analytics pages, nothing formatted, nothing exported.
>
> Thirty days from each channel you sell on is enough to start.
>
> **Want us to look at your store? WhatsApp us today.**

---

## BM 1 — Lawat, tak enquire

> Anda dah tengok page RM499 tu tapi tak contact. Tak apa — ini apa yang
> sebenarnya jadi kalau anda contact.
>
> Anda hantar data 30 hari. Kami balik dengan channel mana yang underperform dan
> berapa nilai gap tu. Lepas tu anda putuskan sama ada kerja bulanan tu berbaloi.
>
> Bacaan datang dulu, dan tak kos apa-apa nak tahu.
>
> **WhatsApp kami sekarang.**

## BM 2 — Dah baca FAQ, tak ambil tindakan

> Masih fikir? Versi jujurnya: kami adviser, bukan agency. Takde sesiapa masuk
> store anda, dan team anda yang jalankan setiap perubahan.
>
> Kalau yang anda perlukan ialah orang buat kerja tu untuk anda, agency lagi
> sesuai dan kami akan cakap terus. Kalau yang anda perlukan ialah tahu kerja
> mana yang penting bulan ni, itu kerja kami.
>
> **Bincangkan store anda dengan kami.**

## BM 3 — Enquiry tak siap

> Anda mula hantar nombor anda tapi berhenti separuh jalan. Kalau sebab leceh —
> screenshot page analytics je dah cukup, tak payah format, tak payah export.
>
> Data 30 hari dari setiap channel anda jual dah cukup untuk mula.
>
> **Nak kami tengok keadaan store anda? WhatsApp sekarang.**
