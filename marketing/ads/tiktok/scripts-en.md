# TikTok — Ad Scripts, English

TikTok punishes anything that opens like an advertisement. These scripts open
on the problem, in the seller's own words, and only name EcomForges once the
point has already been made.

Same constraints as every other channel: PAS, explicit WIIFM, one CTA, no
invented proof, no guarantees. Price is written *from RM499 a month* — true
under either reading of the offer while `marketing/RECONCILIATION.md` is open.

**Format notes.** 20–35 seconds. Talking head or screen-share of a dashboard
with figures blurred. Never show a real client's dashboard, even blurred,
without written consent — a blurred screenshot is still that client's data.

---

## Script 1 — "More traffic won't fix it" (0:25)

**0:00 — Hook, straight to camera**
> If your sales dropped and your first move was to raise the ad budget, stop.

**0:03 — Problem**
> Because there are two very different reasons sales drop. Not enough people
> coming in. Or enough people coming in and not buying.

**0:10 — Agitate**
> Those look identical on a revenue chart. And they need opposite fixes. Spend
> more on the second one and you just pay more per order for the same problem.

**0:18 — Solution + WIIFM**
> Check conversion rate before you touch the budget. If traffic is flat and
> conversion dropped, the money isn't the answer — the page, the offer or the
> reviews are.

**0:24 — CTA**
> We do that read with sellers from RM499 a month. Link in bio.

## Script 2 — "The benchmark you already own" (0:30)

**0:00 — Hook**
> If you sell on Shopee and Lazada, you already own the most useful number in
> your business and you're probably not looking at it.

**0:06 — Problem**
> Same catalogue. Same prices. Same month. Two platforms.

**0:12 — Agitate**
> So if one converts worse than the other, that gap isn't the market and it
> isn't the economy. It's your listing, your platform setup or your campaign
> structure. Most sellers explain that gap away instead of reading it.

**0:22 — Solution + WIIFM**
> Put the two side by side. The stronger platform becomes the target for the
> weaker one, and your problem just got a lot smaller.

**0:28 — CTA**
> That's the kind of read we do. From RM499 a month.

## Script 3 — "Anti-guru" (0:22)

**0:00 — Hook**
> Ecommerce advice online has one answer to everything. Run more ads.

**0:05 — Problem**
> But a store with a conversion problem and a store with a margin problem need
> opposite advice. Same revenue, opposite fix.

**0:13 — Agitate**
> Follow generic advice with the wrong problem and you'll work hard all month
> and move nothing.

**0:18 — CTA**
> Diagnose first. That's the whole job. Link in bio.
