# WhatsApp Education Follow-ups — English

These are **not** outreach. The five outreach templates live in
[`content/outreach-templates.md`](../../../content/outreach-templates.md) and
their compliance rules govern this file too — opt-in, template approval, PDPA,
and the ban risk that comes with volume.

These are for a contact who has already replied at least once and has gone
quiet on the decision rather than on the conversation. The job is to be useful
without asking for anything, so that the next reply comes from interest rather
than pressure.

## Rules for this set

- One idea per message. Two ideas is a newsletter.
- No new offer, no discount, no deadline. Fake urgency is out under
  `brand/copywriting-rules.md` and this is where it is most tempting.
- Ask nothing in three of the four. A follow-up that always ends in a question
  reads as chasing.
- Send at most one per fortnight to the same person.

## 1 — The diagnosis point

```
Hi {{first_name}}, no ask on this one.

Something that comes up often: a store raises the ad budget after a slow
month, and the slow month was conversion, not traffic. The extra spend then
brings more people to the same problem.

Worth checking which one yours was before the next campaign.
```

## 2 — The benchmark point

```
Hi {{first_name}}, one thought from your setup.

If you sell the same catalogue on two platforms, the stronger one is a
benchmark for the weaker one. Same products, same prices, same month — so
whatever the gap is, it is the platform or the listing, not the market.

That comparison costs nothing to run and it narrows the problem fast.
```

## 3 — The prioritisation point

```
Hi {{first_name}}, quick one.

Most sellers are not short of things they could fix. They are short of an
order to fix them in. Ten problems addressed at once is why none of them
visibly moves.

One constraint per month, with a number attached, beats a list every time.
```

## 4 — The open door

```
Hi {{first_name}}, last one from me on this.

If a read on your numbers would be useful at some point, the offer stands and
there is no expiry on it. If it is not the right time, that is a fine answer
and I will leave you to it.

Either way, good luck with the season.
```
