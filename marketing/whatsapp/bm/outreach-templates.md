# WhatsApp Outreach — BM

BM adaptations of the five approved outreach templates in
[`content/outreach-templates.md`](../../../content/outreach-templates.md).

**Read the compliance section of that file before sending anything.** It is the
operational source and it governs these too. In short, and not optional:

- Meta requires opt-in before a business messages a person. None of these are
  cold blasts; each is for a case where a basis to message already exists.
- Templates A, B and D need approval in WhatsApp Manager before first use on the
  Business API.
- A ban takes your existing client conversations with it if it is the same
  number.
- PDPA applies. Opt-out honoured, source of the number defensible.

These are adaptations, not translations. Where a literal translation sounded
stiff, the BM says the same thing the way a Malaysian seller would say it.
Keep them short — under 300 characters where possible. A wall of text reads as
a template in any language.

## A — inbound enquiry, same day

```
Hi {{first_name}}, {{sender_name}} dari EcomForges. Terima kasih sebab
tanya pasal {{brand}}.

Cara paling cepat nak buat ni berguna: hantar data 30 hari dari dashboard
Shopee dan Lazada anda, screenshot pun cukup. Saya akan balik dengan channel
mana yang underperform dan berapa nilainya.

Bahagian tu tiada caj.
```

## B — referral introduction

```
Hi {{first_name}}, {{sender_name}} di sini. {{referrer_name}} cadangkan saya
message anda pasal {{brand}}.

Kami kerja dengan seller Malaysia di Shopee, Lazada dan TikTok. Kami tak run
ads anda atau masuk account anda — kami baca data, tunjuk satu benda yang
paling makan duit bulan ni, dan team anda yang jalankan.

Boleh saya tengok data 30 hari?
```

## C — after they agreed to it

```
Hi {{first_name}}, macam yang dijanjikan.

Hantar bila-bila anda ada: 30 hari Shopee dan Lazada, screenshot page
analytics pun dah cukup. Revenue, visitors, orders, conversion dan ad spend.

Saya baca dan balik dalam dua hari bekerja.
```

## D — re-engagement, at least 30 days quiet

```
Hi {{first_name}}, {{sender_name}} dari EcomForges. Kita ada bincang pasal
{{brand}} bulan {{month}} hari tu.

Bukan nak pitch. Musim campaign dah dekat, dan seller yang buat baik masa tu
ialah yang dah betulkan conversion sebelum traffic masuk, bukan masa traffic
tengah masuk.

Kalau nak saya baca nombor anda, offer tu masih ada. Kalau tak, tak apa.
```

## E — session confirmed

```
Hi {{first_name}}, confirmed untuk {{day}} pukul {{time}}.

Dua benda sebelum tu supaya kita guna sejam tu betul-betul:

1. Data 30 hari dari setiap channel anda jual
2. Satu orang dari pihak anda yang pegang execution antara session

Kita akan tetapkan satu constraint dan tiga langkah. Team anda jalankan, dan
kita ukur sama ada nombor tu bergerak cycle depan.
```
