# Active Campaigns

Seasonal and time-bound campaigns currently running. Each gets its own file.

Move a campaign to `../archive/` when it ends — do not delete it, since a past
campaign's copy and its results are the starting point for the next one.

The always-on campaign is not here; it lives in
[`../evergreen/rm499-always-on.md`](../evergreen/rm499-always-on.md) because it
has no end date.

## Before adding one

A seasonal campaign is the only place in this repository where a real deadline
exists, which makes it the easiest place to slide into fake urgency. The rule
from `../../brand/copywriting-rules.md` still holds: a deadline may be stated
only if it is real. Campaign end dates are real. Stock limits, "last chance"
framing and countdowns on a monthly subscription are not.
